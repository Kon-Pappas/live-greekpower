| K18 | 1 Apr–17 Oct 2026 | **What UCE is.** Full-history calibration (robust regression on 4,800 hours, trained Apr–Aug, tested Sep–Oct, out-of-sample R² 0.84): UCE ≈ 0.19 × metered solar [0.184–0.196] + 0.06 × metered wind [0.048–0.075] + ≈70 MWh/h base [54–96]; shares of UCE: solar 68%, wind 14%, base 15%, residual 3%.
