| K18 | 1 Apr–17 Oct 2026 | **What UCE is.** Full-history calibration (robust regression on 4,800 hours, trained Apr–Aug, tested Sep–Oct, out-of-sample R² 0.84): UCE ≈ 0.19 × metered solar [0.184–0.196] + 0.06 × metered wind [0.048–0.075] + ≈70 MWh/h base [54–96]; shares of UCE: solar 68%, wind 14%, base 15%, residual 3%. Day and night coefficients do not differ; demand adds nothing. Hour-level error: median 119 MWh. Negative hours: 344 (7.2%), almost only at night and at low demand (Sundays, Easter), median −55 MWh; the model expects ≈ +113 there, so ADMIE's estimate falls ≈180 MWh short in those hours. Crete is in neither source. The earlier one-month figures (3% of wind, 3.4% of demand) did not hold | Definition settled (D30); decomposition is a model estimate | open: decomposition on the page (phase 4) |
| K19 | 1 Apr–3 Oct 2026 | **ADMIE and ENTSO-E differ on Greek renewables and load.** Generation: ADMIE (Iptometrics, Apr–Aug) reports RES = 8,389 GWh system + 5,494 GWh network; network RES ≈ RESMV medium voltage (≈3,860) + a low-voltage estimate (≈1,640), close to our UCE (1,982); ADMIE's August report (RES 3,214 GWh, clean 60.7%) includes it. Load (A65, final, 186 days): ENTSO-E is 6.5–8.6% below ADMIE live every month (280–470 GWh/month, 0.7–1.07 × UCE); August 4,986 vs ADMIE report 5,351 GWh (−6.8%). Hour by hour it is **not** "load minus UCE": at midday the gap is ≈700 MWh against ≈1,270 MWh of UCE (about half), at night ≈245 vs ≈125 and in the evening ≈500–570 vs ≈230 (more than UCE). Two separate differences; candidates for the night/evening one: the flow to Crete (contained in ADMIE live load) or network losses.
